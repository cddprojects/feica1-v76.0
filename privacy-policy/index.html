Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Talent Whirl respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you use our website or submit your details.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Skills or experience
  • Any documents you choose to upload

How Your Information Is Used

Your information may be used to:

  • Review your submission
  • Match your profile with available opportunities
  • Contact you about relevant roles
  • Improve how the website functions

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Data Storage and Protection

Your information is stored using reasonable security measures and is only accessible to authorized personnel or partners involved in managing applications.

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your information
  • Update or correct your details
  • Request deletion of your data

To do so, please contact us at: contact@talentwhirl.com

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at: contact@talentwhirl.com
